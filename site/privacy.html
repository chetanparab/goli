Privacy

Nothing to track

Short version: this website collects nothing, and goli keeps your links on your own machine.

This website

goli.theanalogyarchitect.com is a few static pages. There are no cookies, no analytics, no ads, no trackers and no forms. Fonts are served from this site, so your browser doesn't contact Google either. Your light/dark choice is saved in your browser's local storage and never leaves it. Like any website, our host (Cloudflare) processes basic request data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and stop abuse.

The goli app

goli runs on your computer. Your links, settings and logs stay there, in files only your user can read. goli has no account and sends no telemetry. The server answers only your own machine and devices you explicitly allow.

The one exception is AI search, which is off by default. If you turn it on, each search sends your query and a summary of your links (names, descriptions, tags and the host name of each link — configurable) to the AI provider you chose: Anthropic, either through your own Claude Code login or the Claude API. Their privacy terms apply to that data. Opening links never calls AI.
